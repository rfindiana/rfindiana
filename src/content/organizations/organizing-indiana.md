In early 2025, the Committee for Organizing Indiana, a Political Action Committee (PAC), launched Organizing Indiana as a resource hub focused on realizing a common challenge: many of us were doing important work across Indiana, but often in silos, disconnected from each other, duplicating efforts, and missing key opportunities for collective impact.

Our vision is simple: better alignment, not reinvention. Like a solar array, each of our organizations and networks is a vital panel. Some lead long-term campaigns, others power local action. But when the moment calls for statewide mobilization, we need a way to harness and direct all of our collective energy.
