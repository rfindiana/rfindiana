Religious Freedom Indiana is working to protect the separation of church and state, counter Christian nationalism, and defend the religious freedom of all Hoosiers — regardless of faith or no faith at all.

This updates page is where we'll share news, reflections, and resources from our team. Check back regularly or [join our newsletter](/joinus) to get updates delivered to your inbox.
