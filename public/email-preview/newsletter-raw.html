What We're Reading (and Discussing)

If you have time to read only one book about Christian nationalism, Amanda Tyler's book How to End Christian Nationalism would be a great choice. Tyler lays out an 8-step program for learning about Christian nationalism and calling it to account in a loving but firm way, both in private conversations and in the public sphere.

Tyler, the founder of Christians Against Christian Nationalism, encourages folks to not only read books, but to band together to stand against violence, to advocate for the separation of church and state, and to get active. If you'd love to discuss this book with others, see the calendar for two opportunities, one held on Zoom and one in person. Both are led by Steve Ivy.

August Prayer Watch

Inspired by the deep spiritual roots of the civil rights movement, Religious Freedom Indiana invites you to participate in a Prayer Watch on Friday, August 1, from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

During the 1960s, many civil rights leaders and participants—across faith traditions—relied on prayer not just for strength, but for guidance, courage, and unity. Their testimonies remind us that prayer was not an afterthought; it was a foundation.

In that same spirit, we are organizing a time of intentional, shared prayer for religious freedom for all persons of faith and no faith, of compassion for "the least of these," and for mutual respect and kindness for all Hoosiers. People of all faith traditions are warmly welcomed to sign up for a 10-minute prayer slot. Please share the link with anyone you feel would like to participate.

Call Your Legislator

Last week we learned that the federal government is planning to use Camp Atterbury as an ICE detention site. Given what we already know about the deplorable conditions at "Alligator Alcatraz," this raises deep humanitarian concerns. Alarmingly, both the number of detainees and deaths in ICE custody have both risen since Trump took office in January. Contact your legislators and tell them to keep Camp Atterbury solely dedicated to our service members, not ICE.
